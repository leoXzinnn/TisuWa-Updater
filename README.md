## 📱 About TisuWa

**TisuWa** is a modified WhatsApp project created with the goal of
bringing additional privacy controls, customization options and
experimental features to the WhatsApp experience.

The project is the successor to **LXWA**, carrying forward the
experience, ideas and development accumulated throughout the previous
project while introducing a new identity and a new development direction.

> **LXWA → TisuWa**
>
> A new name, a new identity and an evolving project.

TisuWa is designed for users who want greater control over their
messaging experience without giving up the familiar WhatsApp interface.

---

## ✨ Features

TisuWa contains a growing collection of privacy, customization and
utility features.

### 🔒 Privacy

- Hide online status
- Hide typing indicators
- Hide recording indicators
- Privacy controls for contacts
- Privacy controls for groups
- Privacy controls for broadcasts
- Privacy controls for calls
- General privacy controls
- Ghost Mode
- Airplane Mode
- Anti-revoke features
- View Once controls
- Download View Once media
- Download View Once audio
- Additional privacy options through Expert Mode

> Some privacy features may depend on the WhatsApp version and internal
> implementation.

---

### 👤 Contacts Privacy

Dedicated privacy controls for individual contacts.

Possible controls include:

- Read receipts
- Online visibility
- Typing indicators
- Recording indicators
- Status visibility
- Other contact-specific privacy options

---

### 👥 Groups Privacy

Additional privacy controls for group conversations.

TisuWa provides a dedicated area for configuring privacy behavior
inside groups.

---

### 📢 Broadcast Privacy

Dedicated privacy controls for broadcast conversations.

---

### 📞 Calls Privacy

Additional privacy controls related to WhatsApp calls.

---

### 👻 Ghost Mode

Ghost Mode groups privacy-related options into a convenient experience
designed to minimize your visible activity.

Instead of manually changing several settings individually, users can
access privacy-related controls from a centralized location.

---

### ✈️ Airplane Mode

A custom privacy feature designed to provide a quick way of temporarily
changing the app's network-related behavior.

---

### 👁️ View Once

TisuWa includes additional controls for WhatsApp View Once content.

Features include:

- Download View Once media
- Download View Once audio
- Additional View Once controls
- Anti View Once options

View Once audio is handled independently to prevent unintended
interference with normal audio behavior.

---

### 📥 Status Downloader

Download WhatsApp Status content directly through TisuWa.

---

### 💬 New Chat

Quick access to creating a new conversation through the customized
TisuWa interface.

---

### 🔄 Restart WhatsApp

A built-in option to quickly restart the application.

Useful after changing settings that require the application to reload.
